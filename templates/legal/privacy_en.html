What we collect and why

  • Reservation and order: name, email, phone, delivery address, company name and NIF if you give them, gift message and notes. We use them to confirm and deliver your order and to issue the invoice (steps before a contract, the contract itself, and the legal duty to invoice).
  • Pre-launch list: email, name if you give it, what you picked, who it is for and, for companies, the company name and number of boxes. We use it only to tell you when we open and, at most, to ask you one question about what you would buy (consent). Kept until the shop opens and for 12 months at most, deleted earlier on request.
  • Contact form: name, email and your message, to answer you (legitimate interest).
  • Link source: when you arrive through a link with a "src" tag, we store that tag with your cart to know which channel brings visitors. We do not build profiles.

Cookies

Only the cookies the shop needs: the session (cart and checkout), CSRF protection and your language choice. No advertising or analytics cookies.

Who receives your data

The carrier that delivers your order (for example CTT), the bank or payment service you use to pay, and the accountant who keeps the invoices. The shop runs on the seller's own server in Portugal behind Cloudflare, Inc., which processes connection data as an infrastructure provider under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We do not sell data.

How long we keep it

Reservations that are not confirmed: 12 months. Orders and invoices: as long as tax law requires, currently 10 years. Contact messages: 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability by writing to {{ SELLER.email|default:"-" }}. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).
